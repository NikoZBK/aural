# Aural 1.5.1

- Keeps **Delete preset** visible beside **Undo delete** at the bottom of the preset library, without scrolling through the preset details.
- Adds deletion of the selected custom preset and **Undo delete** to the main preset dropdown and menu bar preset menu. Right-clicking a custom preset in the library also offers deletion.
- Shows a notice identifying the deleted or restored preset, with guidance for undoing deletion.

Deleting a saved preset leaves the current EQ unchanged. Built-in presets remain protected. **Undo delete** restores the most recent deletion, including its favorite status, until Aural quits. If its name has been reused, the restored preset receives a unique name.
