AURAL — INSTALLATION

Requires macOS 14.2 or newer. Includes Apple silicon and Intel executables.

1. Drag Aural.app to Applications. If you cannot modify Applications, copy it
   into the Applications folder inside your home folder instead.
2. Eject the disk image and open the installed Aural app.
3. This release is ad-hoc signed and is NOT notarized by Apple. On first launch,
   macOS may block it as an unidentified developer. If you trust this release,
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway after the
   blocked launch, then confirm. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
   Apple instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445
4. Select the stereo output your audio uses and click Start EQ. Allow system
   audio capture when macOS asks. Audio is processed locally, not saved or sent
   to a server. Aural does not change your default sound output.
5. Use the gear menu for Launch at login and Start EQ automatically. Both are
   off for a new installation. If macOS requires login-item approval, use the
   Open Login Items settings button.

Use Stop or Quit to release the equalizer. Closing the window leaves Aural in
the menu bar. Imported AutoEQ files belong to you and are not bundled.

UPDATING
Quit Aural, replace the installed app, then reopen it. Your settings live in
~/Library/Application Support/Aural/settings.json and are preserved. Rebuilt
ad-hoc signed versions may require audio-capture permission again.

UNINSTALLING
Disable Launch at login in Aural's gear menu, quit Aural, and move the app to
Trash. You may remove ~/Library/Application Support/Aural if you also want to
remove your local settings and presets.

Source, releases, and issues: https://github.com/NikoZBK/aural
