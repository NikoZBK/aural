# Aural 2.1.0

- Click **Level** to see live input, post-EQ, and output sample peaks. **Overload hold** records the highest post-EQ sample above 0 dBFS before peak protection. **Limiter hold** records the largest gain reduction. **Reset holds** clears both.
- Choose from five themes in Settings, the View menu, or the headphones menu. Sand adds a warm light palette, and Slate adds a cool dark palette.
- Right-click the Equalizer toolbar to edit as a draft, copy EQ, or paste EQ. The headphones menu keeps playback and presets up front, with other controls grouped under **Sound**, **Open**, and **Theme**.
- Toolbars wrap at higher zoom, and numeric fields line up across the filter editor, stereo controls, and app mixer.
